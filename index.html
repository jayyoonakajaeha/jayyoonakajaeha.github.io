Research Experience

Music and Audio Research Group (MARG)

Seoul National University | Research Intern | Advisor: Kyogu Lee | Jul. - Aug. 2026

Mechanistic Interpretability
  • Analyzed text-token attention and instrument-related activations in MusicGen, identifying early signals associated with violin omission.
  • Tested the causal role of initial tokens through transplantation experiments, showing that replacing early tokens could restore violin generation.
  • Compared attention interventions, activation steering, and classifier-free guidance, reducing omission from 14.4% to 1.4% through guidance adjustment.
